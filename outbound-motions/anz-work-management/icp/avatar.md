## Who they are

- **Company size:** 25–2,000 staff. Narrower than the company-wide
  25–25,000 band — assumption, not confirmed data: ANZ mid-market
  work-management buyers concentrate at the smaller end of that range,
  and 2,000+ staff orgs in ANZ more often already run an enterprise PM
  tool. Revisit this ceiling once Tier A/B conversion data exists.
- **Vertical(s), in priority order:**
  1. **Construction** — primary. Fruition already has a dedicated
     "Construction Product Overview" deck (per
     `knowledge/company/sales-process-sop.md` §4 collateral list), meaning
     there's an existing proof point and sales motion to plug into, not a
     cold vertical.
  2. Professional services
  3. NFP
  (Manufacturing and government are in the company-wide baseline but
  deprioritized for this specific motion's first pass — construction and
  professional services are where "Work Management" as a product
  category — project/task coordination, not CRM or Service — maps most
  directly onto a recognizable pain.)
- **Region:** Australia and New Zealand.
- **Department/buyer:** Operations or PMO — whoever owns project delivery
  visibility, not IT and not the CRM/sales side of the business.

## The pain

- **What's broken today:** project status lives across spreadsheets, email
  threads, and someone's memory. No single source of truth for "where is
  this project at" — the exact pattern
  `sales-process-sop.md`'s discovery guide targets (Problem-stage
  questions 4–7: bottlenecks, manual work nobody owns, things slipping
  through the cracks, spreadsheet/email dependency).
- **What it costs them:** missed deadlines, rework from miscommunication,
  a PM or Ops lead spending hours a week manually chasing status updates
  and rebuilding reports for leadership.
- **Why now (trigger, not generic):** a recent Ops/PM hire (someone new
  is inheriting the mess and has a mandate to fix it), a headcount jump
  that's outgrown the ad hoc process, or a visible project failure
  (missed deadline, client complaint) that made the cost undeniable.

## Buying signals (must each name a real source once sourcing is live)

- Recent Ops/PM/Delivery-lead hire (title change or new-to-company signal)
  — sourced via AI Arc / LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Active job postings for PM/Ops/Coordinator roles — sourced via AI Arc.
- Headcount growth over the last 6–12 months — sourced via Clay
  enrichment / LinkedIn company data.
- Public tool-sprawl evidence (job postings mentioning multiple
  disconnected tools, or G2/Capterra reviews complaining about visibility
  gaps) — sourced via AI Arc / manual research.

## Champion profile

- **Title/seniority:** Operations Manager, PMO Lead, Head of Delivery, or
  equivalent — someone accountable for delivery outcomes, not just a
  coordinator without mandate to change process.
- **What they care about (Need-payoff framing):** getting real-time,
  trustworthy status without chasing it manually — freeing their own time
  and their team's, and giving leadership visibility without a weekly
  manual report.

## Disqualifiers

- Already a Fruition, Orange Growth Labs, or Senzo client.
- Outside ANZ.
- Below ~25 staff (no real PM/Ops function to sell into) or above the
  2,000-staff working ceiling (likely already on an enterprise tool —
  route to a different motion/approach if pursued at all).
- No identifiable Ops/PM-adjacent contact — a company-level "info@" contact
  with no named champion path is not a qualified lead.
